Penning trap explorer: static voltage plus a magnet

The same ring and endcaps as a Paul trap, but with fixed voltages. The static field holds the ion along the axis and pushes it outward sideways. A magnet around the trap bends that outward motion into circles.

Try this

  • Untick “Magnet on”: the static field pushes the ion out sideways into the ring. That is the saddle.
  • Untick “Voltage on”: nothing holds it along the axis any more, and the slow drift disappears.
  • Lower the magnetic field slowly. Below the critical value the ion spirals out: the magnet is too weak to bend the outward push into a circle.
  • Pick “Slow drift only” and raise the voltage: the drift speeds up, because it is driven by the outward electric push.

Units are arbitrary, with charge/mass = 1 and an ideal quadrupole field. Real traps have the three motions far further apart in frequency (for example MHz, hundreds of kHz and kHz); they are squeezed together here so all three are visible at once.
