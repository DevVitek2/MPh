Electrons are drawn as dots so you can count them. Really they are clouds, and they are identical, so which dot moves where is only for illustration. The two spin electrons are drawn with parallel arrows to show they add up to spin 1; the spin state 0 of a spin-1 is a quantum mix that arrows can’t show exactly.
