Two formulas, one number. Multiply matching components and add: axbx + ayby. Or take the lengths and the angle: |a|·|b|·cos θ. The red segment is the shadow b casts on the line of a; the dot product is that shadow's signed length times |a|. At 90° the shadow vanishes and the dot product is zero.

Why it matters for the NV spin

Dot products of the three spin states

Orthogonal states. |+1⟩ = (1,0,0), |0⟩ = (0,1,0) and |−1⟩ = (0,0,1) are at right angles to each other, so every dot product between two different states is 0, and each state with itself gives 1. In quantum mechanics this means: if the spin is surely in |0⟩, a measurement never finds it in |−1⟩.
