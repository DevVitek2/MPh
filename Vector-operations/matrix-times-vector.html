v (drag me) A·v column 1 = A·(1,0) column 2 = A·(0,1)

The one idea to keep: the columns of A are where the basis vectors (1,0) and (0,1) land. A·v takes x copies of column 1 plus y copies of column 2. When A·v stays on the same line as v, v is an eigenvector and the stretch factor is its eigenvalue. The green chip lights up when that happens.
